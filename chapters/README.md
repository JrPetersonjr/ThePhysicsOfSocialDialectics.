# Chapter Template

[This is where new chapters go. Each chapter should explore a different physics concept applied to social interactions.]

## Potential Topics

- **Energy States**: Why people gravitate to low-energy social situations
- **Equilibrium**: How relationships balance out over time
- **Momentum**: Why conflicts escalate and how they slow down
- **Resonance**: When people sync up or clash
- **Entropy**: Social order naturally breaking down
- **Waves**: How ideas spread through groups
- **Pressure**: Social expectations and their effects
- **Friction**: What slows down social change

## Writing Guidelines

Keep it:
- Personal and observational
- Grounded in real examples
- Honest about limitations
- Free of academic jargon
- Written like you're explaining to a friend

Avoid:
- Overreaching conclusions
- Pretending this is science
- Complex terminology without explanation
- Making it sound more formal than it is

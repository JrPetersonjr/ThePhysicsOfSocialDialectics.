# Introduction: Why Physics?

I'm not a physicist. I'm just someone who noticed patterns.

One morning—nothing special about it, really—I was watching two people argue in a coffee shop. The way they leaned in, pulled back, escalated, then suddenly found common ground. It reminded me of something from high school physics: action and reaction, equilibrium, energy transfer.

That got me thinking: what if social interactions follow rules? Not rigid laws, but patterns you can observe and maybe understand better.

This book is my attempt to explore that idea. It's not academic. It's not research. It's just observations from someone who pays too much attention to how people interact.

## What to Expect

- Chapters that build on each other
- Concepts borrowed from physics but applied to social behavior
- Real examples from everyday life
- More questions than answers

## What NOT to Expect

- Mathematical proofs
- Academic citations
- A unified theory of everything
- Pretension

Let's see where this goes.

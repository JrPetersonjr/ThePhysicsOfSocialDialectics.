# The Physics of Social Dialectics

What started as random thoughts on an ordinary morning turned into something worth writing down. This is my attempt to make sense of how social interactions follow patterns—not unlike physics—with their own forces, momentum, and equilibrium states.

## About This Project

This book explores the intersection of social dynamics and physical principles. It's based on my personal observations and reflections, not academic theory. Think of it as notes from someone who got curious about why people act the way they do.

## Structure

The book is organized into chapters that build on each other, starting with basic concepts and moving toward more complex interactions.

## Status

Early development. Chapters are being added as I work through the ideas.
